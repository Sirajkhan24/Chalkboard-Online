Chalkboard Online – Free Digital Blackboard & Chalk Slate

Chalkboard Online is a free web-based virtual blackboard app for teachers, students, artists, and presenters. Draw online with realistic particle chalk textures, customizable brush sizes, shape tools, and felt erasers.

Features

  • No Sign-Up Needed: Start drawing immediately without creating an account.
  • Custom Backgrounds: Upload custom images or choose preset slate, green, math grid, and lined boards.
  • Export to PNG & PDF: Save high-resolution images or PDF documents of your work.
  • Touch & Stylus Compatible: Smooth drawing support across desktops, tablets, and mobile devices.
