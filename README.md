# 🎨 Chalkboard Online

> A free, interactive web-based blackboard drawing application with realistic chalk textures, custom background uploads, and high-resolution PNG/PDF export options.

### 🌐 Live Webpage
👉 **[Play with Chalkboard Online](https://sirajkhan24.github.io/Chalkboard-Online/)**

---

### ✨ Features
- **Realistic Chalk Simulation:** Grainy particle physics with speed and pressure sensitivity.
- **Custom Background Upload:** Use your own image or choose presets (Slate, Green, Math Grid, Lined).
- **Eraser & Clear All:** Dual felt eraser tool and full canvas wiping.
- **Shape & Brush Tools:** Adjustable sizes, opacity, shape tools (Lines, Arrows, Rectangles, Circles).
- **Export Options:** Download high-resolution PNG images or multi-page PDF documents.
- **Zero Friction:** 100% free with no sign-up or installation needed.
